Privacy policy

Hey Claude is a personal voice assistant used only by its owner in their own home.

When the owner connects a Google account, the assistant reads and updates that account's Google Tasks only when the owner asks it to by voice. Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's home computer and are never shared, sold or sent anywhere except Google's own servers.

No data is collected from anyone else. To stop access, remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
