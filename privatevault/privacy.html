PrivateVault Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026 · Developer: Swaub

In short: PrivateVault stores your passwords only on your phone, encrypted. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We never see your passwords or anything else you put in the app.

1. What PrivateVault is

PrivateVault is a password manager for Android that keeps everything on your device. There is no cloud sync and no sign-up.

2. Data we collect

None. PrivateVault does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and it does not create a user account.

3. How your vault is stored

  • Your entries are stored in a database encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher).
  • The database key is protected by your master password using Argon2id key derivation.
  • Attachments are encrypted individually with AES-256-GCM.
  • Android's cloud backup is disabled for the app, so the vault is not copied to Google's servers.
  • Screenshots and screen recording of the app are blocked.

Because we never hold your master password or keys, we cannot recover your vault if you forget your master password and lose your recovery code.

Files you import or export (backups, CSV, attachments) are chosen by you through Android's file picker. The app cannot browse your storage on its own.

6. Autofill

If you enable PrivateVault as your autofill service, it offers matching logins to apps and websites on your device. Autofill runs locally and only while your vault is unlocked. Nothing is transmitted.

7. Backups, transfers and CSV

Encrypted backups (.pvault) are protected with a password you choose and can only be opened with it. You decide where a backup goes, for example another phone, a USB drive or your own cloud storage. CSV exports are not encrypted; the app warns you before creating one.

8. Subscriptions

PrivateVault Pro is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We receive only the subscription status from Google Play, which the app checks on your device. You can cancel any time in the Google Play app.

9. Children

PrivateVault is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

10. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. You can delete all of your data at any time by uninstalling the app.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
